curl2spec

Turn a browser "Copy as cURL" of a login/signup into a ready-to-use 2-account test spec. 100% client-side.
🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. The cURL you paste is parsed locally with JavaScript — nothing is uploaded, logged, or sent anywhere. The cURL, credentials and all, stays on your machine. Safe to paste requests that contain credentials. No network calls, no analytics, no backend.

1 · Paste the login request

In DevTools → Network, log in once, right-click the login request → Copy → Copy as cURL, paste below.

2 · Two accounts (for BOLA/IDOR)

BOLA needs two accounts you control — A owns the data, B is the attacker. Leave blank to emit a single-account spec from the cURL as-is.

3 · Signup request (optional)

For a register spec (auto-mint / reset-recovery), paste the signup request's cURL too.
